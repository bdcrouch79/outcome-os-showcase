# Outcomes OS Product Direction

Outcomes OS is already a working production product. Its next stage is evidence-led improvement, not speculative feature expansion.

## Current focus

- help qualified users reach the first meaningful win quickly
- strengthen the Weekly Execution Loop across planning, action, signals, recovery, and review
- verify activation, first-week return, trial conversion, and retention
- improve onboarding and implementation guidance from observed user friction
- expand permission-backed customer proof
- keep the marketing site and authenticated application synchronized

## Near-term product priorities

- Weekly Outcome Decision Filter
- Drift Recovery Protocol
- Founder Friday Review
- First 14 Days Field Guide
- clearer execution and recovery signals
- stronger insight into repeated patterns over time

## Commercial validation

The current objective is to acquire, observe, and learn from the first 10 paying customers. Pricing, guarantees, annual plans, and major offer changes must follow verified billing behavior and customer evidence.

## Guardrails

- do not turn Outcomes OS into a generic task manager
- do not add features to compensate for weak activation or unclear positioning
- do not publish unsupported productivity or transformation claims
- do not create artificial scarcity or urgency
- do not let public copy drift away from delivered product behavior
